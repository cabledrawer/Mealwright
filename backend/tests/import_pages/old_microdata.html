Granny's Split Pea Soup

By Nell Example

A thick, comforting soup of split peas and smoked ham hock.

Prep: 20 mins Cook: 2 hours Yield: 6 servings

Category: Soup, British

Ingredients

  • 500 g dried green split peas
  • 1 smoked ham hock
  • 2 carrots, diced
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 2 litres water

Method

  1. Rinse the peas.
  2. Put everything into a large pot, bring to the boil and skim.
  3. Simmer for 2 hours, take out the hock, shred the meat and stir it back in.
Per serving: 380 calories, 29g protein, 6g fat
