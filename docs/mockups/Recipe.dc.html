Sambar

Lentil & vegetable stew · 50 min · imported from web
320kcal
16 gprotein
48 gcarbs
7 gfat
11 gfibre

Ingredients

Serves 4
200 g toor dalCupboard
20 g tamarindCupboard
10 shallotsOn list
2 tbsp sambar powderStaple

Method

  1. Rinse the dal and pressure-cook with turmeric until soft.
  2. Soak the tamarind in warm water; squeeze out the pulp.
  3. Cook the shallots and drumsticks in the tamarind water with the sambar powder.
  4. Stir in the dal, simmer, then finish with a mustard-seed tempering.
