Import from Mealie

Bring your recipes and foods across from Mealie in one go. Your Mealwright recipes stay as they are.

How

1Make a backup in MealieIn Mealie: Settings › Backups › Create backup.
2Download itYou get one .zip file. Save it on this phone or computer.
3Choose it hereMealwright reads it and shows you what's inside before anything is added.

What comes across

RecipesSteps, ingredients, nutrition, tags and photos
FoodsMatched to your ingredients; new ones are added
Not brought inMeal plans, shopping lists, users and cookbooks
Mealwright only reads the file you choose. It never connects to Mealie or to the internet.
